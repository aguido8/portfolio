01 / Particle detector research

Research context and role

As a research intern, I assembled and calibrated cosmic-ray detector hardware for atmospheric and environmental physics studies. The work connects physical instrumentation with quantitative data analysis: the quality of the hardware and its calibration influences how confidently measurements can be interpreted.

02 / Particle detector research

Computational analysis

I developed Python scripts to analyze and model 20 years of Earth–Moon orbital-positioning and particle-transmission data. The objective was to understand how geometric and physical conditions influence observed particle transmission over time.

03 / Particle detector research

Particle-based soil moisture detector

I engineered a particle-based soil-moisture detector by evaluating proton transmission rates through soils of varying density. The concept relies on changes in particle transmission through material as a measurable signal, rather than simply using a conventional contact moisture sensor.

Soil density, composition, geometry and moisture content can all affect the measurement. Establishing a calibrated quantitative moisture estimate requires separating these effects and documenting the experimental conditions; no unsupported accuracy figure is claimed here.

Research takeaway

This experience gave me a foundation in instrumentation, experimental calibration, data modeling and translating physical behavior into a measurable quantity. It also informed my later work on telescope structures, where mechanical effects must be understood in relation to instrument performance.
