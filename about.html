About Me

Background, Personal Statement, and Technical Philosophy

Engineering Statement & Career Mission

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at the Georgia Institute of Technology (3.93 overall GPA, 4.00 Major GPA) driven by a deep fascination with complex physical systems, high-speed vehicle design, and hardware-in-the-loop development. My engineering philosophy is built around a relentless iteration cycle: build, test, understand what failed, and improve.

Whether performing ANSYS stress and fatigue simulations on custom racing suspension components, modeling structural assemblies for the Trinity Neutrino Observatory telescope, or analyzing atmospheric particle transmission data using Python, I approach hardware challenges from first principles. I believe that elegant engineering isn't just about making things work, but mastering the underlying thermodynamic, structural, and kinematic physics that dictate why they work.

Beyond CAD modeling and simulation, I thrive in fast-paced fabrication environments. As a candidate Peer Instructor for Georgia Tech's Maker Spaces, I regularly operate and instruct students on manual and CNC mills, 3D printers, laser cutters, and precise shop machinery. Combined with operational logistics experience from Southern Cross Aviation and leadership roles in Theta Chi, I am equipped to bridge high-level analytical engineering with execution on the shop floor.
