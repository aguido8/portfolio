What I work on

My interests span structural and vehicle engineering, mechatronics, computational modeling and scientific instrumentation. I have worked on optical telescope structures, off-road suspension interfaces, autonomous robotics, renewable-energy prototypes and cosmic-ray detectors.

I especially enjoy the point where a calculation changes the geometry of a part or a test reveals that an assumption needs to be revisited. I approach mechanical design through requirements, load paths, interfaces, material behavior and manufacturability.

Beyond engineering coursework

My earlier research internship, FIRST Robotics experience, aviation operations internship and involvement at Georgia Tech have shaped how I work on multidisciplinary teams. I also studied at Georgia Tech Europe in Metz, France, in summer 2026.
